Documentation

File names such as ai.yaml or data/config/ai.yaml mean your own copy in data/config/; the shipped defaults in config/ are read-only (see configuration.md). Most settings can also be changed on the Settings page, or in its Advanced tab.

Start with docker compose up -d --build and the setup wizard at /setup (see setup.md). After the wizard, the tuning guide (open it in a browser) explains the pages and how to adjust what you get, with annotated mock-ups.
